02 / Objective

What are we optimizing for?

The goal is not “more delegates” or “more votes” in isolation. The allocation should improve the operating quality of RARI governance.

01

Active governance

Enough voting power should be held by delegates who are actually available and participating.

02

Quorum resilience

A single Foundation-supported position should not become the obvious point of quorum dependence.

03

Contestability

Power should be able to move toward stronger delegates and away from weak or inactive ones before a crisis.

04

Lower allocator concentration

Foundation support can bootstrap representation without silently turning temporary allocation into permanent incumbency.

07 / Recommendation

Recommended architecture

Use Scenario C as the design target, with Scenario A as the low-friction fallback if the Foundation does not want to revisit legacy allocations now.

Recommended · subject to provenance verification

Standardize Foundation-origin authority. Leave organic authority alone.

The cleanest architecture is a six-member supported cohort at roughly the same Foundation-origin target, reviewed after 90 days and again at six months. Anode’s current Foundation-origin position should be decided by Foundation rather than self-ranked by Anode.

90-day review

No automatic salary
