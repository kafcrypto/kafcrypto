Data & methodology

No fake precision.

Live

Wallet exposure

Direct ERC-20 balances are queried from public Ethereum, Arbitrum and Optimism RPC endpoints for the currently supported governance tokens.

Live

Proposal feeds

Active Snapshot proposals are requested from mapped spaces. If a feed fails, Steward shows the failure instead of filling the gap with sample data.

Heuristic

Priority

Priority combines deadline proximity, governance-impact keywords and detected token exposure. It is decision support, not financial or legal advice.
