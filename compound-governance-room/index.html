Why this exists

Governance should not become an onboarding tax.

Institutional holders can have meaningful governance rights without wanting a full governance department. The missing layer is not another voting interface — it is a clean workflow for attention, accountability and control.

01

Too many surfaces

Forums, proposals, delegates and onchain interfaces fragment the context an institution needs to make a decision.

02

Not everything is material

Routine governance competes with upgrades, treasury decisions and changes that may actually affect institutional exposure.

03

Control cannot be vague

Internal owners, review thresholds and a decision record matter when governance rights sit inside a professional organization.
